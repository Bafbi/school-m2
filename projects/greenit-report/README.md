# greenit-report — group report on responsible digital technology

Deliverable from the GreenIT / "numérique responsable" course: a group report and
its presentation, covering the life-cycle and environmental assessment of digital
services, eco-design, and end-of-life/accessibility questions.

- `ELBAKALI_EUDELINE_FORDELONE_HUBERT_KELMA_POUSSET_report.pdf` — the report
- `..._presentation.pdf` — the defence slides
- `..._zip` — both, as submitted

Team: Elbakali, Eudeline, Fordelone, Hubert, Kelma, Pousset.

`cours/` holds the course slides, `readings/` the papers and case studies the
report draws on (Crawford & Joler on the anatomy of an AI system, and the
GreenIT case study on digital sobriety).
